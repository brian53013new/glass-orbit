# Glass Orbit Design Brief — Desk Orbit Upgrade

**Player promise:** Sit at a tactile space-observatory desk, inspect a real archived NASA clue, and make one understandable resource decision before trying a different plan.

**Feeling:** Curious, capable, and gently challenged—not lectured or buried under control panels.

**Primary verb:** Inspect and choose.

**Secondary verbs:** Look around, focus a terminal, toggle the room language, return, replay.

**First-minute sequence:** Notice the room → turn the view → touch the language dial → focus one terminal → open a card → play it and see the physical resource state change.

**Risk / reward:** Power, life support, shield and evidence cannot all increase at once. A protective move may cost exploration; an observation may cost room stability.

**Failure / recovery:** A run can finish below the learning target; replay is immediate and selects another source-linked course. Tutorial completion never resets a run.

**Non-goals:** Engineering simulation, mission prediction, NASA operational advice, combat, or a separate 2D course site.
